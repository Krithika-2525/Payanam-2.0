# End-to-end hackathon itinerary Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [x]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Turn a selected real city into a complete, editable and portable multi-day itinerary for a ₹0 hackathon.
**Architecture:** Extend the existing FastAPI/React app with bounded public discovery and optimization endpoints, source snapshots, and a private planning JSONB envelope. Separate provider availability from deterministic scheduling and private persistence.
**Tech Stack:** Existing Python/FastAPI/Pydantic/OR-Tools/Postgres and React/TypeScript/MapLibre; no paid dependencies.
**Spec:** docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-07-end-to-end-design.md

## Global Constraints
- ₹0 recurring infrastructure; no paid inference, card or paid resources.
- Preserve existing JWT/RLS/idempotency/consent/account-generation guards and legacy demo.
- 14 generated days, 200 trip items, 20 optional routed stops; 64KB request envelope.
- Common hours interpreted conservatively; unknown travel/fees/accessibility remain visible.
- Public Overpass is a bounded evaluation dependency; no automatic refresh polling.

## Review Focus
- Slow discovery for city A arrives after selecting B: visible city and place results stay aligned.
- Reordering, deleting or moving visits invalidates timing instead of showing stale schedules.
- Required stops closed or unreachable in chosen time remain reported, not silently lost.
- Imported/retained planner metadata must not leak private account state or contain active URLs/code.
- Free services fail independently; sourced snapshot and saved itineraries stay useful.
