# Payanam: a city becomes a journey

The user wants a complete hackathon itinerary app, not just city bookmarks, at ₹0 recurring cost. Selecting a city must expose real nearby hotspots and produce an editable, realistic daily plan. The user explicitly requested autonomous execution without a next-step/design-approval loop; that instruction overrides skill handoff gates. This extends the existing isolated branch and preserves sign-in, ownership, legacy planner and consent protections.

## Journey
Search globally → select city → explore museums, heritage, temples, parks, viewpoints and food → choose dates, interests, pace and travel mode → generate an ordered itinerary → inspect times, distances, opening-hours evidence and daily weather → edit/reorder/move/add/remove visits → track personal costs and packing → save on device or configured cloud → export JSON/calendar and print a trip guide. No login is required to generate or use a trip.

## Real data at zero cost
GeoNames remains global city search. OSM Overpass POST queries provide named hotspots within 8 km of a verified city center, with tagged hours, accessibility, website and Wikipedia source links. Public instances are a small hackathon evaluation dependency, not a scalable service commitment. Bound requests, single-flight, backoff, capped caches, and a bundled licensed snapshot of popular Indian and international destinations support predictable demos. Fresh retrieval is explicit and dated; failures preserve a labelled snapshot, never invent venues. Open-Meteo supplies dated forecasts only within its real forecast horizon (free noncommercial API, CC BY 4.0). No automatic polling, paid inference, or billing-enabled provider.

A no-key geometric travel model uses labelled estimated distances/speeds, not fabricated road directions. Optional openrouteservice free key supplies an explicitly requested driving/walking route for at most 20 stops; API failures retain estimates. Directions links open real mapping apps. No fares, ratings, ticket-price or live-traffic claims.

## Backend planner
New public GET /api/v2/cities/{city_id}/hotspots, GET /api/v2/cities/{city_id}/weather, POST /api/v2/itineraries/generate and POST /api/v2/itineraries/route. Verified city IDs, at most 14 days, 1–10 required stops, interests, relaxed/balanced/packed pace, walking/driving, 06:00–23:00 local daily bounds. OR-Tools optimizes daily routes with visit durations and time windows; named places have stable OSM UUIDs, no duplicate visits across days. Common OSM weekly-hour expressions are interpreted conservatively. Unsupported hours stay unverified; explicit closed days are excluded. Missing required or insufficient stops produce actionable warnings. Lunch/rest blocks and unused time remain visible. Each day's schedule includes return travel and respects its end bound. Weather is separate so a provider outage cannot block planning.

## Persistence and editing
Extend trip metadata with a bounded planning envelope containing city ID, preferences, timed visits keyed by place ID, personal expenses and packing items. No duplicated place objects in metadata. A JSONB migration preserves RLS and optimistic versions. The existing cloud save journal must carry the envelope and real provider snapshots; device/OAuth recovery must retain it. Editing a route invalidates its old generated schedule visibly; a fresh backend schedule for the chosen visits restores timings without dropping a traveler's required stops silently. Do not auto-upload or auto-save private text without existing consent.

## Interface
Keep the Payanam brand and existing discover/trips/translator. Add clear Explore & plan city actions and a focused planner with destination header, preference panel, browsable/filterable hotspot cards, map, day tabs and timed timeline. Route connectors distinguish straight-line estimated links from optional road geometry. Add forecasts, personal budget/expenses, editable packing, printable guide, calendar export, JSON import/share-file, empty/error/retry states. Mobile has list/map tabs and no horizontal overflow; reduced motion and keyboard access remain supported. No promotional fake reviews or photo licensing shortcuts.
